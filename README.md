## About EAT Technologies

EAT Technologies is a technology company based in Nairobi, Kenya. We design and build products across software, AI, and emerging hardware, wherever we see a chance to make advanced technology more accessible, affordable, and useful for people in Africa and beyond.

We are not tied to one category. We follow problems worth solving, then build the product that fits, whether that is a piece of software, an AI system, or a machine that moves through the real world.

**Our belief:** powerful technology should not be limited to people with big budgets, big teams, or expensive equipment. If someone has the idea and the drive, the tools should be within reach.

## Products

### Animora

**AI-native 3D creation studio** · Status: **Live, in active development**

Animora is a desktop 3D creation studio built on a fork of [Blender](https://www.blender.org/), with an AI layer built into the workflow. Describe what you want, and Animora works inside real Blender tools to build it, so everything it produces stays editable by hand.

- **AI inside the studio, not beside it.** The assistant works directly in the 3D scene instead of producing a flat result you can't modify.
- **Native Blender tools only.** Every object, material, and modifier stays fully editable.
- **Built-in verification.** The AI checks its own work against your request before handing it back, enforced in code and not only in prompts.
- **Privacy by design.** API keys and secrets stay server-side and never ship inside the desktop app.

Current version: v1.4.3 · [Try Animora](https://www.animora.tech)

Repository: [github.com/EAT-Technologies/Animora](https://github.com/EAT-Technologies/Animora)

## How we work

1. **Problem first.** We start with a real problem, then choose the technology, not the other way around.
2. **Ship, then refine.** We release early, listen to users, and fix fast.
3. **Honest numbers.** We report real traction and never blur waitlist sign-ups with active users.
4. **Built for modest hardware and budgets.** We design for the equipment and budgets people actually have.
5. **Humans stay in control.** Our tools assist and build; people decide.
6. **Safety and compliance.** Especially for hardware, we build within the law and prioritise safety.
